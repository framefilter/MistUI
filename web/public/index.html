Privacy

        
The MAC address and hostname are the identity hotel
        networks see. Rolling drops the current connection (and any portal
        sign-in), so it happens before joining a network.

        
        
A device profile pairs a matching hardware
        vendor and hostname, so you blend in as that kind of phone rather
        than as an obviously-randomized address.
